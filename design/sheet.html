Interface

One window. The rail holds the six disc tasks and two tools; the pane holds the task. The original and the copy are always shown side by side before anything starts, and every long task shows the disc surface map. Each screen below is the reference for the SPEC section named above it; the same screens are rendered in light and dark in design/mockups.
